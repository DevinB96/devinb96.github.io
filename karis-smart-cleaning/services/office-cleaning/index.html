The checklist

What is included

Our standard checklist for office and commercial cleaning. Add or remove tasks when you request a quote.

Workspaces

  • Desks and clear surfaces wiped
  • Shared equipment and touchpoints sanitised
  • Bins emptied and recycling sorted
  • Glass partitions and doors polished

Kitchens and break areas

  • Worktops, sinks and taps cleaned
  • Appliance exteriors wiped
  • Tables and chairs cleaned
  • Dishwasher loaded or emptied on request

Washrooms

  • Toilets, basins and fittings cleaned
  • Mirrors polished
  • Consumables restocked from your supplies
  • Floors mopped

Floors and common areas

  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Hard floors mopped
  • Entrances and reception tidied

Popular extras

  • Periodic deep cleans
  • Interior and exterior windows
  • Post-event cleans

Questions

Office and commercial cleaning: your questions

Can you clean outside office hours?

Tell us the times that work for your business when you request a quote, and we will confirm the schedule we can offer.

Do you offer one-off commercial cleans?

Yes. Alongside regular contracts, we can quote for one-off deep cleans, for example before an office move or after refurbishment.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

We agree access arrangements with you before the first clean and keep them to the people who need them.
