Service

Deep cleaning in Guildford

A thorough, top-to-bottom clean that resets your home, ideal before a regular routine starts, after a busy season or ahead of a special occasion.

Get a free quote What is included

Illustration

A deep clean goes well beyond the weekly basics. We take the time to clear limescale from taps and shower screens, wash down skirting boards and door frames, clean behind and under furniture we can safely move, and bring kitchens and bathrooms back to their best.

Many people book a deep clean as a fresh start before regular cleaning begins, before guests arrive, or simply when the house needs more than a quick tidy.

Ideal for

  • Homes that have not had a professional clean for a while
  • Getting ready for guests, a celebration or a sale
  • A fresh start before regular cleaning begins

The checklist

What is included

Our standard checklist for deep cleaning. Add or remove tasks when you request a quote.

Kitchen

  • Everything in a regular clean
  • Cupboard fronts and handles degreased
  • Extractor hood and tiles degreased
  • Inside the oven and fridge on request
  • Behind and under movable appliances

Bathrooms

  • Limescale removed from taps, shower heads and screens
  • Grout and sealant scrubbed
  • Toilet cleaned thoroughly, including the base and behind
  • Extractor fans and fittings dusted

Living areas and bedrooms

  • Skirting boards, door frames and doors washed
  • Radiators and light fittings dusted
  • Behind and under movable furniture
  • Window sills and interior glass cleaned

Throughout

  • Switches, sockets and handles cleaned
  • High-level dusting and cobwebs
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

Popular extras

  • Inside kitchen cupboards
  • Interior windows throughout
  • Balcony or patio sweep

Questions

Deep cleaning: your questions

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property. We will give you a realistic estimate with your quote, so you know what to expect on the day.

Can I book a deep clean and then switch to regular cleaning?

Yes. Many people start with a deep clean so that regular visits can maintain the standard from then on. Mention it when you request a quote.

Do you move furniture?

We clean behind and under furniture that can be moved safely. For heavy or fragile items, we will talk it through with you first.
