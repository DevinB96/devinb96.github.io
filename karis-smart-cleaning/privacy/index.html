Privacy notice

How Karis Smart Cleaning Services Ltd collects and uses your personal information.

Last updated 18 October 2026

Who we are

Karis Smart Cleaning Services Ltd (“we”, “us”) is the controller of the personal information described in this notice. We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 15459605, registered office 2 Hillside Court, Harvey Road, Guildford, England, GU1 3NA.

To contact us about your data, write to us at our registered office: 2 Hillside Court, Harvey Road, Guildford, England, GU1 3NA, or message us on Facebook.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (if given), postcode, the type of property and clean, your preferred date and anything you add in the message box. If you become a customer, we also hold the information needed to arrange and carry out your cleans, such as your address, access arrangements and payment records.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote. This is a step you have asked us to take before entering into a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To provide and manage the services you book. This is necessary to perform our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep business and tax records. This is a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business, who process information on our behalf and only as we instruct. These include our website host, the service that delivers website form submissions to our inbox, and our email provider. Some providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

If you request a quote but do not become a customer, we delete your enquiry within 12 months. Customer records are kept for as long as we provide services to you and then for up to six years, to meet our legal and tax obligations.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to ask for a copy in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
