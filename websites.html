The small print

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

01What do I own?

Your domain is registered in your company’s name and stays yours. Your content is yours, and so are your website files once the build fee is paid.

02How do I pay?

The build fee is paid in full before your site goes live. The monthly fee is collected by Direct Debit from the month you go live. We’ll confirm both figures in writing before any work starts.

03Can I cancel?

Yes. There’s no minimum term: cancel any time with 30 days’ notice. You’ll get your website files and help moving your domain; your new host reconnects forms and tracking.

04What if I need more than small edits?

Anything beyond the monthly hour, such as new pages, a redesign or features like online booking, is quoted as a fixed price. No work starts until you approve it. Unused edit time doesn’t roll over.

05I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can rebuild it on the same plan, keeping your domain and anything that’s working well. If your site is fine but enquiries go unanswered, our Never Miss an Enquiry package for trades firms may suit you better.
